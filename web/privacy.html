U'r Invente ni huduma ya Tembo Cloud ("sisi") inayomsaidia mteja ("wewe") kutengeneza kadi za mwaliko na kuzituma kwa waalikwa kwa SMS, WhatsApp na email. Sera hii inaeleza taarifa tunazokusanya, kwa nini, na haki zako, kwa kufuata Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi ya Tanzania, 2022.

Taarifa tunazokusanya

  • Akaunti yako: jina, jina la biashara (si lazima), namba ya simu, email na nenosiri (linahifadhiwa kwa njia fiche; hatulioni).
  • Matukio na waalikwa: maelezo ya tukio (jina, tarehe, ukumbi), na kwa kila mwalikwa unayemuongeza: jina, namba ya simu, email, kundi, idadi ya viti, jibu la RSVP na muda wa kuingia mlangoni.
  • Contacts: ukichagua kuongeza waalikwa kutoka contacts za simu, tunasoma tu contacts unazochagua. Hatupakii orodha nzima ya contacts zako.
  • Picha: design ya kadi unayopakia na kadi zinazotengenezwa kwa kila mwalikwa.
  • Kamera: inatumika kuskani QR code za kadi mlangoni. Picha za kamera hazihifadhiwi wala kutumwa.
  • Malipo: idadi ya mialiko uliyonunua, kiasi, njia ya malipo, namba ya simu ya malipo na namba ya muamala. Hatukusanyi namba za kadi za benki.
  • Utumaji: kumbukumbu za ujumbe uliotumwa (njia, hali, muda) ili kukuonyesha historia na kukutoza mara moja tu kwa kila mwalikwa.

Tunavyotumia taarifa hizi

  • Kukufungulia akaunti na kukuwezesha kuingia.
  • Kutengeneza kadi na kuzituma kwa waalikwa wako kwa njia unazochagua.
  • Kuonyesha majibu ya RSVP na kuthibitisha wageni mlangoni.
  • Kusimamia salio la mialiko na kuthibitisha malipo.
  • Kutoa msaada na kulinda huduma dhidi ya matumizi mabaya.

Hatuuzi taarifa zako wala za waalikwa wako, na hatuzitumii kwa matangazo.

Taarifa za waalikwa

Taarifa za waalikwa unaziweka wewe kama mwenyeji wa tukio, na unawajibika kuwa na ruhusa ya kuwatumia mialiko. Sisi tunazitumia kwa niaba yako tu, kutuma mialiko na kupokea majibu yao. Mwalikwa anayetaka taarifa zake ziondolewe anaweza kuwasiliana na mwenyeji au na sisi.

Watoa huduma tunaoshirikiana nao

  • Supabase: database, akaunti na hifadhi ya picha.
  • Beem Africa au Africa's Talking: kutuma SMS.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Cloud API): kutuma ujumbe wa WhatsApp.
  • Resend: kutuma email.
  • GitHub Pages: kuhifadhi web app na kurasa hizi.

Kila mmoja anapata taarifa zinazohitajika kwa kazi yake tu (mf. mtoa huduma wa SMS anapata namba ya simu na ujumbe). Baadhi ya watoa huduma hawa wanahifadhi data nje ya Tanzania.

Muda tunaohifadhi taarifa

Tunahifadhi taarifa zako mpaka utakapofuta akaunti. Ukifuta akaunti, akaunti yako, matukio, waalikwa, kadi, salio na historia ya malipo vinafutwa mara moja. Nakala za akiba (backups) zinafutwa zenyewe ndani ya siku 30.

Haki zako

  • Kuona na kurekebisha taarifa zako ndani ya app (Akaunti → Wasifu wako).
  • Kufuta akaunti na data yako yote: ndani ya app (Akaunti → Futa akaunti) au kwa ukurasa huu.
  • Kuuliza swali au kulalamika kuhusu matumizi ya taarifa zako kupitia __SUPPORT_CONTACT__.

Usalama

Mawasiliano yote yanapita kwa HTTPS, na kila mteja anaona data yake tu (row level security). Hakuna mfumo usio na hatari kabisa, kwa hiyo tumia nenosiri imara na usilishiriki.

Watoto

Huduma hii ni kwa watu wenye umri wa miaka 18 au zaidi wanaoandaa matukio. Hatukusanyi taarifa za watoto kwa makusudi.

Mabadiliko

Tukibadilisha sera hii, tutaweka tarehe mpya hapa juu, na mabadiliko makubwa tutayatangaza ndani ya app.

Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026

U'r Invente is a service of Tembo Cloud ("we") that helps customers ("you") design invitation cards and send them to guests by SMS, WhatsApp and email. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights, in line with Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, optional business name, phone number, email and password (stored hashed; we cannot read it).
  • Events and guests: event details, and for each guest you add: name, phone, email, group, seats, RSVP answer and check-in time.
  • Contacts: only the contacts you pick when importing guests. We never upload your whole address book.
  • Photos: card designs you upload and the personalised cards generated for guests.
  • Camera: used to scan card QR codes at the door. Camera images are not stored or sent.
  • Payments: credits bought, amount, payment method, payer phone and transaction reference. We do not collect bank card numbers.
  • Delivery logs: which messages were sent, on which channel and when, so we can show history and charge each guest only once.

How we use it

To run your account, generate and deliver invitations on the channels you choose, collect RSVPs, check guests in, manage your credit balance and payments, provide support and prevent abuse. We do not sell your data or your guests' data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Guest data

As the event host you add your guests' details and are responsible for having permission to invite them. We process that data on your behalf only to send invitations and receive responses. Guests may ask the host or us to remove their details.

Service providers

Supabase (database, authentication, file storage), Beem Africa or Africa's Talking (SMS), Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (WhatsApp), Resend (email) and GitHub Pages (web hosting). Each receives only what it needs for its task. Some of them store data outside Tanzania.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting your account immediately removes the account, events, guests, cards, remaining credits and payment history. Backups expire automatically within 30 days. You can delete your account in the app (Akaunti → Futa akaunti) or via the account deletion page.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS, and each customer can access only their own data (row level security).

Children

The service is intended for adults (18+) organising events. We do not knowingly collect children's data.
