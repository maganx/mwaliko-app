KISWAHILI

Kufuta akaunti ya U'r Invente

Unaweza kufuta akaunti yako ya U'r Invente (ya Tembo Cloud) na data yake yote wakati wowote.

Njia ya 1: ndani ya app (haraka zaidi)

  1. Fungua U'r Invente kwenye simu au kwenye web app, kisha ingia.
  2. Nenda Akaunti → Futa akaunti.
  3. Andika FUTA kuthibitisha, kisha bonyeza Futa kabisa.

Akaunti inafutwa papo hapo.

Njia ya 2: kwa ujumbe

Kama huwezi kuingia, tuma ombi kupitia __SUPPORT_CONTACT__ ukiandika "Futa akaunti" na email au namba ya simu uliyotumia kujisajili. Tutathibitisha kuwa akaunti ni yako na kuifuta ndani ya siku 7.

Kinachofutwa

  • Akaunti na wasifu wako (jina, biashara, simu, email, nenosiri).
  • Matukio yote, waalikwa wao, majibu ya RSVP na kumbukumbu za check-in.
  • Design za kadi ulizopakia na kadi za waalikwa.
  • Salio la mialiko lililobaki, historia ya matumizi na historia ya malipo.
  • Kumbukumbu za ujumbe uliotumwa.

Kinachobaki na kwa muda gani

Hakuna data inayobaki kwenye mfumo baada ya kufuta. Nakala za akiba (backups) zinafutwa zenyewe ndani ya siku 30. Ujumbe uliokwisha kutumwa kwa waalikwa unabaki kwenye simu au email zao.

ENGLISH

Delete your U'r Invente account

You can delete your U'r Invente account (by Tembo Cloud) and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open U'r Invente on your phone or in the web app and sign in.
  2. Go to Akaunti (Account) → Futa akaunti (Delete account).
  3. Type FUTA to confirm and tap Futa kabisa (Delete permanently).

The account is deleted immediately.

Option 2: by request

If you cannot sign in, contact __SUPPORT_CONTACT__ with the subject "Delete account" and the email or phone number you registered with. We will verify ownership and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your account and profile; all events, guests, RSVP answers and check-ins; uploaded designs and generated cards; remaining credits, credit history and payment history; delivery logs. Nothing is retained after deletion; backups expire within 30 days. Messages already delivered to guests remain on their devices.
